Privacy policy

Applies to the two private Google sign-in apps named "Hermes Agent". Last updated 25 November 2026.

Who uses them

Only the owner of this GitHub account, with his own Google accounts. The apps are not offered to the public.

What they access

Read-only access to the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar (the gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly permissions). They cannot send, change or delete anything.

How the data is used and kept

The data is read on the owner's own computer for his own admin, such as matching receipts to his bookkeeping and reading his calendar. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
